Eastern Market – Individual vs Clustered Routes

Layers

Use the map control (top-right) to toggle:

  • Clustered routes – EM truck routes (per cluster color)
  • Individual routes – each vendor drives to EM
  • Overlap – clustered – thicker where more clustered routes share a road
  • Overlap – individual – thicker where more individual routes share a road

Cluster Legend

Notes

Base line width is the same as the routes (5 px). Each additional overlapping route makes the white overlap lines noticeably thicker, so you can see where traffic concentrates in the original vendor network vs the EM truck network.
